## Judgement calls
1. **Shahu's release** is in the passive ("was set free and sent home"), because sources differ on whether Azam Shah or Bahadur Shah freed him.
2. **No "shortest reign" claim** for Rafi-ud-Darajat. I couldn't confirm it was the shortest, so he "reigned for just over three months".
3. **No "opium addict" label** for Shah Jahan II, as instructed.
4. **Violence is shown only through objects:** a cloth, an empty throne, snuffed candles, prison bars, a falling banner and a cup. No bodies, blood or blinding on screen.
5. **"Blinded and killed"** is spoken, because it is in your verbatim cold open, but never shown.
6. **Added a re-hook beat at 3:11** ("1717 · A SMALL FAVOUR"). Without it there would have been a 64-second gap between beats, and the rule is 60 at most.
7. **The Owen quote is verbatim from the prompt.** The voice says "Bahadur Shah the First, he wrote, was the last emperor…". On screen it reads: Bahadur Shah I "was the last Emperor of whom anything favourable can be said." — Sidney Owen, quoted in R. Ahir, p. 67.
8. **The Bengal foreshadow** is only a pulsing crimson outline. The map stays green, as the prompt asks.
9. **ahir.pdf was not in the repo,** so book facts come from the prompt's fact list.
10. **Captions use real Whisper word timing.** The Whisper download worked in this session, so the fallback wasn't needed. I still added `tools/words_fit.py` to the kit for future sessions.
