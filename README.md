# ClearCut History — The Fall of the Mughals

Series repo for the 12-episode YouTube series (16:9 cut-paper documentaries made in code).

- `FALL_OF_MUGHALS_PROMPTS.md` — Part A (series rules) and the 12 episode prompts.
- `_mughals-kit/` — the shared engine and series kit (built in Episode 1). See its README.
- `episodes/epNN-<slug>/` — one project per episode (script, shots, timing data, packaging, review sheet).
  Rendered videos, audio and thumbnails are not committed; they are delivered in the session chat.

Episodes: 1 · The Kingmakers Who Killed an Emperor (`episodes/ep01-kingmakers`).
